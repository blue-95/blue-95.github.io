AI-Driven Operational Strategy

Scaling a scientific ecosystem of 40 discrete companies requires a flawless digital infrastructure. This project focused on building an automated, analytics-driven framework to replace manual documentation, transforming tenant engagement into actionable, real-time metrics.

The Challenge

Facility scaling and grant acquisition were severely bottlenecked by manual Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and disconnected qualitative data. With over 20 active laboratories, the administrative overhead was pulling focus away from core research. We needed a centralized way to automate workflows and visualize operational health for executive stakeholders.

The Architecture & Workflow

I designed a dual-layered approach combining workflow automation with high-level business intelligence:

  • Workflow Automation: Engineered AI-driven workflows to automate routine SOPs across the laboratory network, significantly reducing manual data entry and human error.
  • Data Aggregation: Centralized disconnected tenant data into a cohesive digital pipeline.
  • Executive Dashboards: Developed interactive Power BI dashboards that translated raw operational data into clear, actionable metrics regarding facility usage and tenant engagement.

The Business Impact

The implementation of this digital strategy achieved a 60% sustained adoption rate of data-driven workflows among the research teams. Furthermore, by providing transparent, real-time operational metrics, the framework directly enabled and supported a $50M grant funding pipeline.
